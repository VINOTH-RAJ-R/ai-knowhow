# Regulated categories: market-specific reference

**When to open this file:** only when the prompt is actually in one of the categories and markets below. Read only the matching section and leave the rest out of the answer. If the prompt's category or market isn't listed, research the current rules (step 2b) or tell the user to get the mandatory elements from their compliance team. Never borrow rules from another market or category.

Rules change. Quote each rule with its source, tell the user to confirm the current text, and never invent a limit, deadline, disclaimer wording or legal line. Required lines are set as real type or overlaid in the edit, never generated by an image or video model. End the answer with a "compliance review needed" list.

## India: mutual funds and investment products (SEBI, AMFI)

**Advertising** (SEBI advertisement code and circulars of 2008 and 2017, AMFI best-practice circular 109 of 2023; SEBI is reportedly moving mutual fund ads into a common advertisement code for intermediaries, so confirm which text applies):
- **Standard warning,** verbatim and legible: "Mutual Fund investments are subject to market risks, read all scheme related documents carefully." This is the advertisement-code wording. Older circulars (2003, 2008) and many web pages still show "read the offer document carefully before investing", which is outdated.
  - Vernacular ads carry it in that language.
  - Its type size is in proportion to the rest of the ad (AMFI).
  - In video and audio it is both shown and spoken, for at least 5 seconds (SEBI 2008). Never speed it up to fit.
- **Scheme and AMC:** the scheme name and AMC name, with the scheme's risk-o-meter prominently visible near the scheme name.
- **Launch (NFO) creatives** normally also carry the scheme category, the open and close dates and a CTA. Confirm them with compliance.
- **Never include:** celebrities in product ads, testimonials, rankings, return promises, projections or illustrations of future returns, "best", "guaranteed" or "assured". No imagery implying a return.
- **Performance data:** only in the prescribed format (SEBI 2017: set periods, plan type, risk-o-meter). None for schemes under one year old. Leave performance blocks to compliance.
- **Influencers** must be SEBI-registered or education-only, and disclosure labels follow ASCI rules.
- **Filing:** ads are filed with the regulator after issue.

**Engineering:**
- **NAV cut-off:** depends on scheme type, the request time and, for purchases, when the funds are realised. Verify against the current SEBI master circular.
- **Recurring mandates:** UPI AutoPay and NACH limits change, so keep them in configuration. NACH rejects differ from returns, and a transaction missing from a response file is not a success.
- **RTA integration** (CAMS, KFintech): ask for the interface spec, and plan reconciliation of units and folios.
- **Cyber resilience:** SEBI's cybersecurity framework (CSCRF) applies to AMCs and RTAs. Flag it rather than summarise it.

## India: banking and payments (RBI, NPCI)
- **Data storage:** payment-system data is stored only in India (RBI FAQ on storage of payment system data).
- **Outsourcing of IT services** (RBI Master Direction, 2023): contract SLAs, audit rights, incident reporting and an exit plan. Accountability stays with the bank.
- **Recurring e-mandates:** need a registered mandate and a pre-debit notification. Per-transaction limits change, so read them from configuration and confirm the current RBI circular.
- **Lending ads:** must not contradict the Key Fact Statement and the all-inclusive APR.

## India: crypto and virtual digital assets (ASCI, 2022)
- **Disclaimer:** ASCI's prescribed text, on a plain background with voice-over. In video it stays on screen for at least 5 seconds.
- **Banned words:** never use "currency", "securities", "custodian" or "depositories".
- **Never:** promise returns, or feature minors.

## Card payments (any market)
- **PCI DSS:** keep card data off your servers with tokenisation or hosted fields. Never log card number, CVV or OTP.

## AI-generated content
- Label AI-generated ad content where the platform requires it (Meta, TikTok, YouTube) or local law does (India's IT Rules amendments of 2026 reportedly require labelling of synthetic content). Confirm the current policy.
