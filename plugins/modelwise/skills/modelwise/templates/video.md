# Template: video generation (video.generate)

**Status (2026-10):** neither vendor in this registry currently offers a video generator. OpenAI shut down the Sora 2 API on 2026-09-24, and Anthropic does not generate video. This template still gives you a strong, generator-agnostic prompt and shot list (it's based on OpenAI's archived Sora guide, which applies to most text-to-video tools). Ranked recommendations for specific generators will come in a future registry update. Until then, name the common ones as unranked options (below) and use the text model from the routing to write and refine the prompts.

## Fill these slots
| Slot | Why it matters | Default if missing |
|---|---|---|
| PURPOSE | Ad, b-roll, explainer, social short, music video | Ask |
| PLATFORM | Aspect ratio and length: 9:16 Reels/TikTok, 16:9 YouTube, 1:1 | Ask |
| DURATION | Total length and number of shots | Short clips (4-8s each) |
| STORY | What happens, beginning → end | (required) |
| SUBJECT | Who/what is on screen; keep a consistent description | (required) |
| LOOK | Visual style, era, color palette (3-5 color anchors), film stock | Natural, contemporary, grounded daylight (cinematic only for entertainment, gaming or festival work) |
| CAMERA | Per shot: framing, angle, one movement, lens | Per shot |
| LIGHT | Light sources and direction, kept consistent across shots | Soft key light |
| SOUND | Dialogue (short lines, labeled speakers), ambience, music | Ambience only |
| REFERENCE | First-frame image or reference images | None |

## Per-shot prompt skeleton
```
[Prose description of the scene: where we are, who is there, what it feels like]

Cinematography:
  Camera shot: <framing + angle, e.g. "medium close-up, low angle">
  Movement: <ONE move, e.g. "slow dolly-in">
  Lens: <e.g. "35mm, shallow depth of field">
  Mood: <...>
Lighting: <sources, direction, color temperature>
Palette: <3-5 color anchors>
Actions (beats):
  - <beat 1, with timing, e.g. "0-2s: she lifts the cup">
  - <beat 2>
Dialogue:
  <SPEAKER>: "<short line>"
Background sound: <ambience / music cue>
Style: <film stock / era / animation style>
```
Set duration, resolution and aspect ratio in the generator's **settings**, not in the prompt text.

## The producer's layer (ads, shorts and explainers; see `guides/practice-content.md`)
Before any shot prompt, write a short production brief. It covers:
- objective and KPI, and one proposition
- the hook in the first 3 seconds
- where the brand appears (early, and said aloud as well as shown)
- one CTA on an end card
- the deliverables (9:16 master plus 4:5 and 16:9, cut-downs, hook variants)

Then write a two-column script (picture | audio, about 2.5 words per second of voice-over) and a numbered shot list. The film must work muted (burned-in captions, at most 7 words per card) and with sound. Keep text inside the platform's safe zones.

**Never generate** text, logos, UI, real product footage, numbers or legal disclaimers in the video model. They're overlaid in the edit. A mandatory warning in a regulated category is shown and spoken for as long as the regulator requires (`guides/regulated.md` for the markets it covers).

## Where to generate, and how to finish (generate-then-edit)
Claude and ChatGPT write the plan; they don't make the clips. Tell the user exactly this:
1. **Generate each shot** in a video generator: Google Gemini Omni Flash (Gemini API and Gemini app; GA Veo models on Google's enterprise platform; the Veo 3.1 Preview API shut down on 2026-10-22), Kling or Runway. These are unranked and unpriced here, so check which one your plan includes. Paste **one shot prompt at a time**. Set the aspect ratio (9:16 for Reels and Stories) and the clip length (4–8 seconds) in the tool's settings. Generate several takes per shot and keep the best; professionals often need 10 or more per usable shot.
2. **Lock the look first** if the tool supports it: a reference image of the product or character (a still from ChatGPT image generation works), or first and last frames, so the shots match.
3. **Edit** in CapCut, Canva or Premiere:
   - put the clips in order and trim to the script's timings
   - add the voice-over (record it, or use a text-to-speech tool) and music you have rights to
   - add captions and text cards from the overlay list, in your real font
   - add the logo, end card and any legal lines
4. **Export one version per placement:** 9:16 (Reels, Stories), 4:5 (Instagram and Facebook feed), 16:9 or 1:1 (LinkedIn, YouTube). Check each against the platform's safe zones.
5. **Label AI-generated content** where the platform or local law requires it.

## Notes for Google Gemini Omni Flash (from Google's video docs, October 2026)
- Output is multi-shot by default. Write "single continuous shot, no scene cuts" when you want one take.
- Timing can be written as timecodes: `[0-3s] … [3-6s] …`.
- Audio is generated by default. Describe it ("gentle breeze, distant birdsong"), or write "No dialogue" to suppress speech.
- There's no separate negative-prompt field. Write exclusions as plain constraints in the prompt.
- For image-to-video, describe the camera, the subject's motion and the environment's effects, not "make it move".

## Taste for film
Use the industry's taste direction and craft recipe from `guides/creative-taste.md` for casting, light, grade and camera moves. Give each film one signature craft decision. Write "documentary realism, natural light" rather than "cinematic", unless the industry is entertainment, gaming or festival work.

## Rules that work across generators
- **One camera move, one subject action, one lighting recipe per shot.** Complex shots misfire.
- **Shorter clips follow instructions better.** Two stitched 4-second shots often beat one 8-second shot.
- **Keep continuity**: repeat the exact subject description, palette and light sources in every shot.
- **Iterate one variable at a time.** If a shot misfires, simplify it, then add complexity back.
- Use a first-frame/reference image to lock the look of a character or product.

## Example (illustrative: shows the structure, never copy its specifics)
**Basic:** "a 15 second ad for my running shoes"
**Structured shot list (9:16, 3 shots × 5s):**
1. *Dawn street*: a runner's feet in neon-green shoes hitting wet asphalt. Camera: ground-level close-up, tracking alongside. Light: blue pre-dawn with orange streetlamp rim light. Beats: 0-2s stride, 2-5s splash in a puddle in slow motion.
2. *Bridge*: the runner crosses a city bridge as the sun rises. Camera: wide shot, slow crane up. Palette: deep blue, sunrise orange, neon green. Beats: steady run, sun flares behind.
3. *Product*: the shoe rotates on a wet black surface. Camera: macro, slow orbit. Text overlay added in editing, not generated: "RUN BEFORE THE CITY WAKES."

## Token & cost savers
- Lock the script and shot list with a cheap text model before spending on video generation. Video is by far the most expensive modality per attempt.
- Generate the cheapest preview resolution and shortest duration first; upscale and extend only the keepers.
- Add text overlays and music in editing, not in generation.
