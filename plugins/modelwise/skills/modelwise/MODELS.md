## Where the facts come from
Every price, limit and effort level on a card is taken from the vendor's own documentation, linked in the card's `sources`, and checked on the card's `verified` date. Blog posts, leaks and social media are not used. Sources by site: `developers.openai.com`, `platform.claude.com`.

Fit ratings (0–5 per task) are curated judgments built from those docs, not benchmark scores. A daily GitHub Action (`tools/watch_models.py`) checks the vendors' model overview pages for IDs the registry doesn't know, cards past their retirement date, and cards not re-verified in 45 days.

Prices are list prices in USD per million tokens (input / output) unless another unit is shown.
