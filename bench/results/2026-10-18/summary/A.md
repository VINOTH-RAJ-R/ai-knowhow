**Q3 Logistics Platform Update: Executive Summary**

- **Decision needed: ₹2.1 crore capex for a second Chennai sorter.** The hub currently has one sorter, so a single breakdown stops it. The team expects the investment to pay back in 14 months.
- **Volume up 18% QoQ to 2.4M parcels.** The new Bengaluru grocery partner added 310K parcels, about 85% of the quarter's growth.
- **On-time delivery fell from 96.1% to 93.4%, and the cause is fixed.** Almost all of the drop came from a nine-day backlog after the Chennai sorter broke down in August. The sorter was replaced on Sep 3, and Chennai has been above 95% on-time since mid-September.
- **Cost and quality both improved.** Cost per parcel fell from ₹41 to ₹37 (about 10%), helped by route batching going live in five cities. Complaints dropped from 22 to 17 per 10K parcels.
- **Risks to watch.** Monthly driver attrition rose from 5% to 7%. Exit interviews point to the new shift rota, so HR will pilot the old rota in two cities in October. Separately, the Pune hub launch moves from November to January because the landlord handed over the site late.
