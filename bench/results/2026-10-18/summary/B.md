- Shipment volume grew 18% quarter over quarter to 2.4 million parcels, driven mainly by the new Bengaluru grocery partner, which added 310,000 parcels. The Pune hub launch has moved from November to January because the landlord delayed handover.

- On-time delivery fell from 96.1% to 93.4%, almost entirely due to the Chennai hub, where an August sorter breakdown caused a nine-day backlog. The sorter was replaced on September 3, and Chennai on-time delivery has been back above 95% since mid-September.

- Cost per parcel fell from ₹41 to ₹37, helped by route batching, now live in five cities. Customer complaints per 10,000 parcels fell from 22 to 17.

- Driver attrition rose to 7% monthly from 5%, and exit interviews point to the new shift rota. HR proposes reverting to the old rota in two pilot cities in October.

- Approval requested: ₹2.1 crore capex for a second Chennai sorter to remove the single point of failure, with an expected payback of 14 months.
